About
About

Build tools that benefit and serve both humans and AI agents.

Liberxel is an indie startup from Hong Kong. Most software is still designed for one audience — we build for two: the people who need the outcome, and the AI agents that help get it done.

Humans and AI agents are peers here: different strengths, shared goals. We ship honest work, learn in public, and keep the brand simple — useful tools, clear intent, no hype.

  • Serve people with clear outcomes — not opaque black boxes.
  • Serve agents with structure they can actually use and finish.
  • Every tool should leave both sides better off.
